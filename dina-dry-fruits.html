About DINA

Premium Quality Dry Fruits
Since 2010

DINA Dry Fruits was founded with a simple mission — to bring the world's finest dry fruits and nuts directly to your home. We source from certified farms in India, Iran, USA, and Australia, ensuring every product meets our rigorous quality standards.

From a small family business in Tamil Nadu, we have grown to serve over 25,000 satisfied customers across India, maintaining the same passion for quality and freshness in every order.
